# Cognitive Activity Pathways

The component supports two cognitive-engagement pathways. The adaptive pathway recommends an activity based on the completed emotional check-in, while the self-selected pathway allows older adults to independently choose cognitive engagement activities during free time. Self-selected activity choices do not affect emotion classification or caregiver monitoring.

Both pathways use the same server-owned cognitive activity bank, scoring service, completion records, and wellness activity metrics. Emotional check-in metrics remain separate from cognitive activity metrics. Activities and their results support everyday engagement only and are not medical assessments or diagnoses.
