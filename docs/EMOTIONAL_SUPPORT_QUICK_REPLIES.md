# Emotional Support Quick Replies

Quick Replies were introduced as an accessibility aid to reduce typing effort for older adults. They are curated, balanced, and question-specific. Users can always provide unrestricted text or voice responses, and all input methods are processed through the same NLP and contextual interpretation pipeline.

Quick Replies are deterministic question-bank metadata. They are not generated by an LLM, do not encode a target emotion or selector weight, and do not use a separate submission path. No claim is made that they improve emotion-classification accuracy.

## Coverage

Twenty-four active, polarity-friendly assessment questions have two or three concise replies. Nine narrative questions intentionally have none:

- `open_mind_today`
- `neutral_daily_engagement`
- `lonely_daily_engagement`
- `sad_share_difficult`
- `anger_frustrating_today`
- `happy_special_memory`
- `happy_daily_engagement`
- `happy_energy_today`
- `cognitive_engagement_today`

These remain unrestricted because a canned answer would reduce conversational quality or fail to answer the narrative prompt meaningfully.
